About Us

Two decades of balanced education in Kitikifumba

Alheib Primary School was established in 2002 in Kitikifumba, Kira Town Council, Wakiso District. As a government-sponsored school and orphanage centre, we are affiliated with the International Islamic Charitable Organization (IICO) based in the State of Kuwait, which sponsors our pupils so they learn free of charge.

  • Established 2002, serving the community for over 20 years
  • Government-sponsored with IICO Kuwait affiliation
  • Zero school fees thanks to generous sponsors
  • Uganda curriculum (English) plus Islamic curriculum (Arabic)
