Enrolment

We welcome every child, regardless of background

Alheib Primary School provides free, quality education to orphaned and needy children in Kitikifumba. Thanks to our sponsors at the International Islamic Charitable Organization (IICO) of Kuwait, pupils pay zero school fees.

  • Primary 1 to Primary 7 (Uganda curriculum)
  • Islamic studies and Quran memorisation
  • Day school with a caring, disciplined environment
  • Zero school fees for sponsored pupils

How To Apply

A simple process for parents and guardians

1. Visit the School

Come to our campus in Kitikifumba, Kira Town Council, and speak with the administration about your child.

2. Submit Details

Provide the child's birth certificate or recommendation letter, and any documents showing orphan or needy status for sponsorship.

3. Enrol & Welcome

Once approved, your child is welcomed into a class and issued with school materials and a uniform programme.
Documents Required

What to bring when applying

To complete enrolment, parents or guardians should bring the following documents for the child:

Birth Certificate

Official copy for age verification.

Recommendation Letter

From a religious or community leader, if available.

Orphan / Needy Status

Evidence of orphaning or need helps our sponsorship team assist.

Medical Records

Immunisation and health records if available.

About
